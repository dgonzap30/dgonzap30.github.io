02 / Method

Correctness is a product feature.

I care about the unglamorous parts because they determine whether a product becomes dependable infrastructure or another prototype people work around.

01

Find operating truth

Map the real actors, data, constraints, handoffs, and incentives before picking a solution.

02

Specify the whole system

Make permissions, integrations, failure behavior, acceptance criteria, and ownership explicit.

03

Ship evidence

Move in small, verifiable increments with source gates, observability, and production proof.

04

Own the aftermath

Design recovery paths and stay accountable after launch, when the system meets the real world.
