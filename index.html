About

A bit of context.

I split my time between Madison and San Diego. I grew up between Mexico and the US, picked up code in high school, and have been shipping production software since my sophomore year at UW-Madison.

In 2024 my brother Mariano and I started LOJIK Labs — a tiny studio for vertical software in niches the big players don't bother with. I lead engineering on every product we ship. Mariano runs ops, partnerships, and the parts of the business I'm not good at.

When I'm not in a terminal: producing in Logic Pro X, playing golf when Wisconsin lets me, and thinking about how a two-person team can build like a twenty-person one. I read a lot of systems design and indie-hacker postmortems, and I take Spanish more seriously than my résumé would suggest.

I graduate this month and I'm open to founding-engineer roles, contract product builds, and serious side projects. The easiest way to find out if we'd work well together is to email me.
