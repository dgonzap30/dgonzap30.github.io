Maestro · Private beta

Coaching evidence, kept inspectable.

A music-production workspace that turns session signals into material a producer can inspect.

Engineering case study · Capture in preparation

The engineering decision

Real-time ingest and audio analysis must cross plugin, native, and interface boundaries without turning incomplete evidence into a confident suggestion. Maestro keeps the evidence state visible so coaching can be understood in context.

What is available now

An approved synthetic-session capture is being prepared. It will be added when it can show a real product state without exposing private session material. There is no runnable or recorded demo on this page yet.

Any future capture will use synthetic input; it will not claim autonomous DAW edits or model quality.
