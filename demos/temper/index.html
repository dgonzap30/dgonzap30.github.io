Engineering detail: what's working, what's still in progress

Local drafts and commit-time validation keep an impossible schedule visible instead of silently starting it. Every plan revision carries who changed it and why, and a pending Coach proposal is shown as a proposal to accept or decline, never an already-applied change.

What's working

  • A day view that ties readiness, check-ins, meals and training into one read
  • A meal plan accepted for the week, tracked meal by meal against its targets
  • A Coach proposal — three concrete setting deltas with reasoning — shown for review, not auto-applied
  • A full revision ledger: every plan setting shows who changed it and why (Coach vs. you), not just its current value

Still in progress

  • No HealthKit or WHOOP sync — readiness, sleep, weight, food and training numbers are synthetic fixture data
  • Captured in an isolated iOS Simulator build; physical-device acceptance is incomplete
  • Coach is a scripted plan/proposal surface, not an autonomous or AI-powered agent
